Last time the bump switch was a button: each press added 10 to your drive velocity. Today it is a sensor on the front of the robot. It tells the robot "I just hit something!"

1 Check your robot

2 Predict what will happen

Your program will make the robot do these 4 things, over and over:

Write your prediction: After the robot turns, which way will it drive? What will happen when it hits the next wall? ______________________________

3 Build the program

Open VEXcode Blocks. Build it in three parts. The block with the green outline is the new one in that part.

Part A: Start slowly (you used this block last time)

when started
set drive velocity to 50 %

Part B: Drive until the bump

when started
set drive velocity to 50 %
forever
drive forward
wait until BumperA pressed?

"wait until" makes the robot keep driving until the bump switch is pressed.

Part C: React to the bump

when started
set drive velocity to 50 %
forever
drive forward
wait until BumperA pressed?
stop driving
drive reverse for 150 mm
turn right for 90 degrees
Heads up: the pictures are drawn to look like VEXcode blocks. Your block names might be a little different (for example your bumper may have another name or port). Look for the blocks with the same words and use your own bumper.
Why "forever"? Everything inside it repeats again and again, so the robot keeps exploring.

4 Test it and fix it

Download the program to your robot and run it. Record what you see:

TestWhat should happenWhat happened?
Start facing a wallDrives, bumps, backs up, turns
Start in a cornerBounces around, does not get stuck for long
Change velocity from 50 to 80Same moves, but faster

Something wrong? Try these fixes:

ProblemTry this
Robot never stops at the wallCheck the bumper port matches the port in your block. Make sure the switch hits the wall first.
Robot drives past the wallLower the velocity, and check the switch sticks out at the front.
Robot turns the wrong amountChange the degrees a little (try 80 or 100) and test again.
Robot gets stuck in a cornerMake "reverse" longer (try 250 mm) so it has room to turn.

5 Challenge: Level Up!

Level 2: Change the turn. Change right to left, or change 90 to 45 degrees. Predict first: how will the robot's path change? Then test it.

Level 3: Stop after 5 bumps. Replace forever with repeat 5. What does the robot do after the 5th bump?
